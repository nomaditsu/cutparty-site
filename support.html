Support

CutParty turns your video clips into one cleaned, color-graded, silence-stripped video. Here is how to get going and how to reach us.

Getting started

  • Import your video files (drag them onto the sidebar, or click Import to pick them).
  • Choose your edits per clip: Cut Silences, Reduce Noise, Normalize Audio, Color Grade.
  • Export as a single stitched video or as individual files, at the size you choose.

Requirements

  • macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
  • Apple Silicon Mac

FAQ

Where do my exports go?

To the folder you pick in the Export panel (Desktop by default).

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally on your Mac. CutParty collects no data and needs no account.

Which formats are supported?

Common video formats such as MP4 and MOV.

An export failed — what do I do?

Use the in-app option to save a bug report and email it to us (below), and we will take a look.

Contact

Email support@cutparty.com and we will help as soon as we can.
