Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

CutParty is a macOS app that cleans, edits, and stitches your video clips. Your privacy is simple to explain, because CutParty does its work entirely on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. CutParty does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party data SDKs.

Your videos

The clips you import and the videos you export are processed locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. CutParty does not require a network connection to do its work.

Purchases

If CutParty offers any optional in-app purchase, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information.

Diagnostics

CutParty does not send crash reports or usage data to us. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers through Apple's standard system setting, Apple provides aggregated, anonymized information; CutParty does not collect it directly.

Children

CutParty does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@cutparty.com.
