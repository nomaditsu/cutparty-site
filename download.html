Activate your key

Where to paste it.

1. Open CutParty and choose CutParty ▸ Settings… (or press ⌘,).

2. On the General tab, find CutParty Pro and click Activate License…

3. Paste the license key from your receipt email and click Activate. The sheet says "CutParty Pro active".

You can also click the Upgrade button in the window's top bar while Pro is not active.

Good to know

Questions about your key.

One key, three Macs

Each purchase gives you one license key that works on up to three of your Macs.

Moving to a new Mac

On the old Mac, open Settings ▸ General ▸ CutParty Pro ▸ Manage License… and click Deactivate This Mac. That frees the seat for your new Mac.

Lost your key?

Open your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, or email support@cutparty.com and we will help.

Offline use

CutParty checks your key now and then. If you are offline, Pro stays on until the next successful check.
