CutParty is a pre-editor — a first pass that gets your footage clean before your real edit even begins. Cut silences, clear noise, level loudness, grade color, all in one pass.

What comes out is production-ready: bring it into your editor for the creative cuts, or, if clean was all you needed, publish it in one shot.

Three steps

From raw footage to production-ready.

No timeline, no scrubbing, no plugins to install. Everything happens in one window.

Import

Drag your video files onto the sidebar, or click Import to pick them. CutParty lines them up for you.

Choose your edits

Toggle Cut Silences, Reduce Noise, Normalize Audio, and Color Grade — per clip.

Export

Get one stitched video, or every clip as its own file, at the size you choose.
